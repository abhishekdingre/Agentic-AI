# Pharmacokinetics and Metabolism of AXN-2401 in Preclinical Species

## Overview

This paper reports the absorption, distribution, metabolism, and excretion (ADME) profile of
AXN-2401 across mouse, rat, dog, and cynomolgus monkey, supporting dose selection for subsequent
toxicology and first-in-human studies.

## Absorption and distribution

AXN-2401 showed good oral bioavailability across species (F = 62-74%), with peak plasma
concentrations reached 1.5-2.5 hours post-dose. Plasma protein binding was high but consistent
across species (94-97% bound), and lung-to-plasma partition ratios of 3-5x were observed in rat
tissue distribution studies, favorable for a pulmonary-fibrosis indication.

## Metabolism

AXN-2401 is metabolized primarily via CYP3A4-mediated oxidation, with two major circulating
metabolites, neither of which retains meaningful CRB3 antagonist activity (<5% of parent potency
in the reporter assay). No metabolites unique to human hepatocytes were identified in a
cross-species metabolite comparison, supporting the use of standard preclinical species for
toxicology.

## Elimination

Terminal half-life ranged from 8 hours in mouse to approximately 14 hours in cynomolgus monkey,
supporting once- or twice-daily oral dosing in humans. Elimination was predominantly hepatobiliary,
with less than 5% of an administered dose recovered unchanged in urine across species.

## Relevance to dose selection

The species PK profile summarized here was used, together with the efficacious exposure identified
in the bleomycin mouse efficacy study, to project a human dose range and to inform the design of
the 28-day rat toxicology study reported separately.

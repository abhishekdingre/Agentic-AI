# Elevated CRB3 Expression in Lung Tissue from PF-7 Patients: An Immunohistochemistry Survey

## Purpose

To confirm at the protein level, in an independent patient cohort, that CRB3 expression is
selectively elevated in PF-7 lung tissue relative to other fibrotic and non-fibrotic lung disease,
extending the biomarker-based classification described in earlier work defining the PF-7 subtype.

## Methods

Archival lung biopsy sections from 88 patients (26 PF-7, 34 non-PF-7 idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis,
28 non-fibrotic disease controls) were stained for CRB3 by immunohistochemistry and scored blind to
clinical classification by two independent pathologists.

## Results

Mean CRB3 IHC score was significantly higher in PF-7 tissue (mean H-score 187) than in non-PF-7
idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis tissue (mean H-score 94, p<0.001) or non-fibrotic controls (mean
H-score 12, p<0.001). CRB3-positive staining localized predominantly to myofibroblast foci at the
fibrotic-normal tissue interface, consistent with the pattern described previously in the CRB3/FBX9
axis review. Inter-observer agreement on CRB3 scoring was high (weighted kappa 0.84).

## Discussion

This independent cohort confirms elevated CRB3 protein expression specifically in PF-7 lung tissue,
reinforcing CRB3 as a rational, biologically-supported target for pharmacological intervention in
this patient subgroup. Because staining was restricted to lung tissue, these data speak only to
pulmonary disease and make no claims about CRB3 expression in kidney or other organs.

# Regulatory Strategy Briefing: AXN-2401 in PF-7

## Purpose

This briefing summarizes the current regulatory strategy for AXN-2401 in PF-7, for internal
portfolio governance review.

## Regulatory designations

AXN-2401 has been granted orphan drug designation for the PF-7 subtype of pulmonary fibrosis,
based on the biomarker-defined prevalence estimate of approximately 15% of the broader idiopathic
pulmonary fibrosis population. A request for breakthrough therapy designation was submitted
following the Phase 2a proof-of-concept efficacy result (120 mL FVC treatment difference at 24
weeks) and is currently under agency review.

## Registration strategy

The current registration strategy anticipates a single pivotal Phase 3 study (AXN-2401-301,
described in the associated clinical trial protocol summary) using time to first acute exacerbation
or death as the primary endpoint, with FVC change as a key secondary endpoint, consistent with
recent agency guidance for pulmonary fibrosis subtype-specific development programs.

## Companion diagnostic

Because patient identification relies on the CRB3 IHC plus serum FBX9 biomarker panel, the
regulatory strategy includes co-development of this panel as a companion diagnostic, submitted in
parallel with the therapeutic product application.

## Scope

This briefing addresses only the PF-7 pulmonary fibrosis regulatory pathway. No regulatory strategy
work has been undertaken, and no filings are planned, for any non-pulmonary indication, including
renal fibrosis; such an indication is outside the current program scope and would require a
separate, not-yet-initiated development program.

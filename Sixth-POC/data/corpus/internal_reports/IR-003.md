# Formulation and CMC Development Report for AXN-2401 Tablets

## Purpose

This report summarizes formulation and chemistry-manufacturing-controls (CMC) development work
supporting selection of the clinical tablet formulation for AXN-2401.

## Solid form selection

Early formulation work with the AXN-2401 free base identified hygroscopicity and inconsistent
crystallinity as manufacturing risks. Based on solid-state screening, the crystalline hydrochloride
salt (Form A, described in the associated patent filing on crystalline salt forms) was selected as
the development form due to superior physical stability and consistent crystallinity across
manufacturing batches.

## Tablet formulation

A direct-compression tablet formulation containing AXN-2401 Form A, microcrystalline cellulose,
lactose monohydrate, and magnesium stearate was developed and used across the Phase 1 through Phase
2b clinical studies. Dissolution testing showed greater than 85% drug release within 30 minutes
across all clinical tablet strengths (25, 50, 75, 100, and 150 mg).

## Stability

Clinical tablet batches met all release specifications after 24 months of storage at long-term
storage conditions (25degC/60% relative humidity) and after 6 months at accelerated conditions
(40degC/75% relative humidity), supporting a proposed shelf life of 24 months for the commercial
formulation.

## Conclusion

The Form A tablet formulation developed here has supported all clinical studies to date and is
proposed as the basis for the commercial formulation, pending confirmatory stability data from
ongoing Phase 3 clinical batches.

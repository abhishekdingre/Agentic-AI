# 28-Day Rat Toxicology Study Report for AXN-2401 (Corrected Re-Analysis)

**STATUS: CURRENT — this version supersedes IR-002-v1 and corrects its NOAEL determination.**

## Background on this revision

The original 28-day rat toxicology report for AXN-2401 (IR-002-v1) concluded a NOAEL of 150
mg/kg/day based on an initial histopathology read. During a routine quality-control re-review of
the full histopathology slide set ahead of IND filing, the study pathologist identified minimal-to-
mild hepatocellular hypertrophy in the 150 mg/kg/day dose group that had been under-called in the
original read and not flagged as test-article-related. This report presents the corrected
re-analysis.

## Corrected findings

Blinded re-review of liver histopathology slides by a second independent pathologist confirmed
minimal-to-mild, centrilobular hepatocellular hypertrophy in 7 of 10 animals (both sexes) at 150
mg/kg/day, consistent with an adaptive test-article-related change. No comparable finding was
present at 75 mg/kg/day or below. All other endpoints (clinical chemistry, hematology, and
histopathology of kidney, lung, and heart) were unchanged from the original analysis and showed no
test-article-related findings at any dose.

## Corrected no-observed-adverse-effect-level (NOAEL)

Based on the corrected histopathology finding at 150 mg/kg/day, the NOAEL for AXN-2401 in this
28-day rat study is revised to **75 mg/kg/day**. The 150 mg/kg/day dose is now classified as the
lowest-observed-adverse-effect-level (LOAEL) for this study.

## Impact and disposition

This corrected NOAEL of 75 mg/kg/day is the value that must be used for all subsequent safety
margin calculations for AXN-2401, including first-in-human dose selection. IR-002-v1 is formally
superseded by this report and must not be cited for the NOAEL value going forward.

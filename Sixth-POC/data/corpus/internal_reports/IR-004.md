# PK/PD Modeling Report: Dose Projection for CRB3 Target Engagement

## Purpose

This report describes the population pharmacokinetic-pharmacodynamic (PK/PD) model used to project
human doses of AXN-2401 expected to achieve sustained CRB3 target engagement, informing dose
selection ahead of the Phase 1 first-in-human study and subsequent Phase 2 dose selection.

## Model inputs

The model integrated preclinical receptor occupancy data from the bleomycin mouse efficacy study,
cross-species PK scaling from the preclinical ADME package, and in vitro potency (IC50 18 nM in the
CRB3 reporter assay) to project the human plasma exposure needed to maintain greater than 80%
receptor occupancy for at least 20 of 24 hours at steady state.

## Projections

The model projected that a 100-150 mg once-daily oral dose in humans would achieve the target
receptor occupancy threshold at steady state, consistent with the biomarker suppression later
observed clinically at 100-150 mg in the Phase 1b and Phase 2a studies (serum FBX9 reduction of
54-61% at these doses).

## Model validation

Retrospective comparison of model-predicted serum FBX9 suppression against observed Phase 1b data
showed good agreement (predicted 58% suppression at 150 mg vs observed 61%), supporting the
model's use for ongoing Phase 3 dose confirmation and potential future pediatric or hepatic-impairment
dose-adjustment modeling.

## Conclusion

This PK/PD model supported selection of the 100-150 mg dose range for clinical evaluation and
provided a prospectively validated link between preclinical target engagement and clinical
biomarker response.

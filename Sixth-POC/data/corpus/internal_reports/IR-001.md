# Internal Pharmacology Report: CRB3 Target Validation Program Summary

## Purpose

This internal report summarizes the pharmacology team's target validation package for CRB3,
compiled to support the go/no-go decision to advance AXN-2401 into IND-enabling studies.

## Summary of internal evidence

Internal receptor binding studies confirmed AXN-2401 binds the CRB3 extracellular domain with a Kd
of 3.2 nM, consistent with external medicinal chemistry publications. Internal CRB3 knockout mouse
colonies bred in-house showed no baseline pulmonary, renal, or hepatic phenotype at 12 months of
age, supporting a favorable predicted safety margin for pathway inhibition. Internal
gene-expression analysis of banked human lung tissue samples (n=41) confirmed elevated CRB3
transcript levels in fibrotic regions relative to matched non-fibrotic regions from the same
tissue donors, corroborating external immunohistochemistry findings.

## Internal efficacy replication

The pharmacology team independently replicated the bleomycin mouse efficacy result at the 30 mg/kg
dose (hydroxyproline reduction of 54% relative to vehicle, consistent with the externally published
58% figure), confirming reproducibility of the primary efficacy claim ahead of IND-enabling
toxicology.

## Recommendation

Based on convergent internal and external target validation evidence, the pharmacology team
recommended proceeding to IND-enabling toxicology studies for AXN-2401, which are reported in the
accompanying 28-day rat toxicology study report.

# 28-Day Rat Toxicology Study Report for AXN-2401

**STATUS: SUPERSEDED — see IR-002-v2 for the corrected re-analysis. Do not cite this version.**

## Study design

Sprague-Dawley rats (n=10/sex/group) were dosed orally once daily with AXN-2401 at 0, 25, 75, or
150 mg/kg/day for 28 consecutive days, with a 14-day recovery period in a subset of animals.
Standard toxicology endpoints were assessed, including clinical observations, body weight, food
consumption, clinical chemistry, hematology, and histopathology of major organs.

## Findings (original analysis)

No test-article-related mortality occurred at any dose. Clinical chemistry and hematology showed
no dose-related changes across groups. Histopathology review at the time of this report did not
identify test-article-related findings in liver, kidney, lung, or heart at any dose level,
including the high dose of 150 mg/kg/day.

## No-observed-adverse-effect-level (NOAEL)

Based on the absence of test-article-related findings at any dose tested in this analysis, the
NOAEL for AXN-2401 in this 28-day rat study was established at **150 mg/kg/day**, the highest dose
tested.

## Note

This NOAEL determination was subsequently found to require correction after an expanded
histopathology re-review; see IR-002-v2 for the corrected NOAEL and the basis for the correction.
This version (v1) should not be used for any dose-margin calculation.

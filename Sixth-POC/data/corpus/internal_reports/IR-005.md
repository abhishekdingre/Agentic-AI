# Competitive Landscape Analysis: CRB3-Targeted Antifibrotics

## Purpose

This report reviews the external competitive landscape for CRB3-targeted therapies as of the most
recent portfolio review, to inform program prioritization and lifecycle strategy for AXN-2401.

## Landscape summary

As of this review, no external competitor program targeting CRB3 directly has been identified in
public disclosures, patent filings, or clinical trial registries. AXN-2401 remains, to the internal
competitive intelligence team's knowledge, the most clinically advanced CRB3-targeted compound
disclosed to date, currently in Phase 3 development for PF-7.

## Adjacent mechanisms

Several external programs targeting adjacent or parallel antifibrotic mechanisms (autotaxin
inhibition, integrin antagonism, senolytic combinations) are in Phase 1-2 development for
idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis broadly, without patient stratification by CRB3 or FBX9 biomarker
status. None of these programs are known to overlap mechanistically with CRB3 antagonism.

## Strategic implication

The absence of a direct CRB3-targeted competitor, combined with the biomarker-based patient
stratification strategy protected by the internal biomarker panel patent filing, is assessed as
providing AXN-2401 a multi-year first-in-class and potentially best-in-class window in the PF-7
subtype, pending successful Phase 3 readout.

## Caveat

This analysis reflects competitive intelligence available at the time of this review and does not
constitute a guarantee that no competing program is in earlier-stage, non-disclosed development.

# AXN-2401-202: A Phase 2 Cardiac Safety Substudy (Holter ECG and QTc Analysis)

## Background

An independent academic in vitro electrophysiology report described a hERG channel inhibition
signal for AXN-2401 and raised the possibility of a narrow cardiac safety margin. This substudy was
conducted within the ongoing Phase 2 PF-7 program to prospectively assess cardiac safety using
continuous ECG monitoring in patients receiving therapeutic doses of AXN-2401.

## Design

52 PF-7 patients receiving AXN-2401 150 mg once daily underwent 12-lead Holter ECG monitoring
continuously for 24 hours at baseline, week 4, and week 12, with time-matched QTc measurement
against a placebo-controlled reference group (n=26) enrolled in parallel.

## Results

Mean placebo-corrected change in QTcF from baseline was 2.1 ms at week 4 and 1.8 ms at week 12 in
the AXN-2401 group, with the upper bound of the two-sided 90% confidence interval remaining below
10 ms at all timepoints. No patient in either group met the threshold for QTcF prolongation of
regulatory concern (>60 ms change from baseline, or absolute QTcF >500 ms). No episodes of
ventricular arrhythmia were recorded on continuous Holter monitoring in either group. No cardiac
serious adverse events occurred during the substudy.

## Conclusions

In this prospective, continuously-monitored substudy conducted specifically in response to the
published in vitro hERG signal, AXN-2401 at therapeutic doses did not produce clinically meaningful
QTc prolongation or any observed arrhythmic signal, providing clinical-level cardiac safety data
that is reassuring at the doses studied. This substudy's ECG-based clinical findings did not
themselves re-test the in vitro hERG IC50 value reported in the academic literature; the
discrepancy between the in vitro signal and the absence of a clinical QTc effect observed here is
not resolved by this substudy and is discussed further in the internal cardiac safety review.

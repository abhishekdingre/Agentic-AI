# AXN-2401-203: A Phase 2b Dose-Ranging Study in PF-7

## Design

Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, 24-week, four-arm dose-ranging study of AXN-2401 (50,
100, or 150 mg once daily) versus placebo in 210 biomarker-confirmed PF-7 patients, intended to
characterize the dose-response relationship for FVC decline and identify the dose for Phase 3.

## Efficacy results

FVC decline at week 24 was 132 mL (placebo), 88 mL (50 mg), 58 mL (100 mg), and 42 mL (150 mg),
demonstrating a clear dose-response relationship. The 100 mg and 150 mg doses both achieved
statistically significant separation from placebo (p<0.01 for both), while the 50 mg dose showed a
numerical but not statistically significant improvement.

## Safety results

Adverse event rates were similar across the 50 and 100 mg doses and placebo. The 150 mg dose showed
a modestly higher rate of mild transaminase elevations (8% vs 2-3% in lower-dose and placebo arms),
though all cases resolved without intervention and none met criteria for drug-induced liver injury.

## Dose selection rationale

Based on the efficacy plateau between 100 and 150 mg and the modestly higher transaminase signal at
150 mg, 100 mg once daily was selected as the recommended Phase 3 dose, balancing efficacy and
tolerability.

## Conclusions

This study confirms a dose-dependent slowing of FVC decline across the tested dose range and
identifies 100 mg once daily as the Phase 3 dose for further evaluation in PF-7.

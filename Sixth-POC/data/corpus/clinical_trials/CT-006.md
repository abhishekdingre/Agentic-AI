# AXN-2401-204: A Phase 2 Open-Label Extension, Long-Term Safety in PF-7

## Design

Open-label extension study enrolling patients who completed the Phase 2a and Phase 2b PF-7 studies,
continuing AXN-2401 at 100 mg once daily (the selected Phase 3 dose) for up to 96 additional weeks,
to characterize long-term safety and durability of effect.

## Safety results

Across 187 patients with a median of 68 weeks of additional exposure, the long-term safety profile
was consistent with earlier studies: mild gastrointestinal adverse events and headache remained the
most common, with no new safety signals identified. Transaminase elevations remained infrequent
(4% of patients) and were transient. No cardiac serious adverse events were reported during the
extension period, and periodic ECG monitoring continued to show no clinically meaningful QTc
changes from baseline, consistent with the dedicated cardiac safety substudy.

## Durability of effect

Among patients who continued treatment, the rate of FVC decline observed during the parent studies
was generally maintained during the extension period, without evidence of attenuating benefit over
time.

## Conclusions

Long-term exposure to AXN-2401 at the Phase 3 dose was well tolerated over an additional 96 weeks
with no new safety signals, including no cardiac signal on continued ECG monitoring, supporting the
overall benefit-risk profile of AXN-2401 at 100 mg once daily in PF-7.

# AXN-2401-102: A Phase 1b Multiple-Ascending-Dose Study with CRB3 Pathway Biomarkers in PF-7 Patients

## Design

Open-label, multiple-ascending-dose study of oral AXN-2401 (25, 75, and 150 mg once daily for 28
days) in 36 patients with biomarker-confirmed PF-7 (elevated serum FBX9 and CRB3 lung tissue
staining per the diagnostic panel established in prior work), designed to assess safety,
tolerability, and target engagement in the intended patient population.

## Pharmacodynamic (biomarker) results

Serum FBX9 concentration decreased in a dose-dependent manner from baseline, with the 150 mg cohort
showing a mean 61% reduction in serum FBX9 by day 28, compared to a 12% reduction in the 25 mg
cohort. This dose-dependent biomarker suppression is consistent with the pharmacodynamic
relationship observed preclinically in the bleomycin mouse efficacy model, and supports that
AXN-2401 engages the intended CRB3/FBX9 pathway in PF-7 patients.

## Safety results

Adverse events were predominantly mild-to-moderate and included headache, nausea, and mild
transaminase elevation in two patients at the 150 mg dose (both resolved without dose
discontinuation). No serious adverse events were reported. No patient discontinued due to a
treatment-related adverse event.

## Conclusions

AXN-2401 produced dose-dependent target engagement (serum FBX9 suppression) in PF-7 patients with
an acceptable short-term safety profile, supporting progression to a proof-of-concept efficacy
study.

# AXN-2401-301: Phase 3 Study Design and Protocol Summary in PF-7 (Ongoing, Interim)

## Program scope

The AXN-2401 clinical development program is designed exclusively around pulmonary indications,
specifically the PF-7 molecular subtype of pulmonary fibrosis. No renal fibrosis, hepatic fibrosis,
or other extrapulmonary fibrotic indication is currently included in the clinical development plan
for AXN-2401, and no studies of AXN-2401 in renal fibrosis of any kind have been conducted or are
planned as of this protocol summary.

## Design

Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, 52-week, multinational Phase 3 study of AXN-2401 100
mg once daily versus placebo in an planned enrollment of 480 biomarker-confirmed PF-7 patients.
Primary endpoint is time to first acute exacerbation or all-cause mortality; key secondary endpoint
is change in FVC from baseline to week 52. Patients are identified for enrollment using the CRB3
IHC plus serum FBX9 diagnostic panel established in earlier biomarker validation work.

## Status

As of this interim summary, the study is actively enrolling and has not reached its primary
analysis. No efficacy or safety readout is available at this time; enrollment and safety monitoring
are ongoing under an independent data safety monitoring board, which includes continued ECG
monitoring given the cardiac safety questions raised in the published literature.

## Note on scope for future readers

Questions about renal fibrosis efficacy, or about any AXN-2401 indication outside pulmonary
fibrosis, cannot be answered from this program: no such studies exist in the AXN-2401 clinical
development plan.

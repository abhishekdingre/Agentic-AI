# AXN-2401-101: A Phase 1 First-in-Human Safety and Pharmacokinetics Study in Healthy Volunteers

## Design

Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, single- and multiple-ascending-dose study of oral
AXN-2401 in 72 healthy adult volunteers. Single doses ranged from 5 to 400 mg; multiple-ascending
doses of 25, 75, and 150 mg once daily were given for 14 days.

## Safety results

AXN-2401 was well tolerated across all single-ascending-dose cohorts, with no serious adverse
events and no dose-limiting toxicities. In the multiple-ascending-dose cohorts, the most common
adverse events were mild headache and mild nausea, occurring at similar frequency to placebo. No
clinically significant changes in vital signs, standard 12-lead ECG intervals (including QTc), or
laboratory safety parameters were observed at any dose tested, up to and including 150 mg once
daily for 14 days.

## Pharmacokinetics

AXN-2401 exhibited dose-proportional pharmacokinetics across the tested range, with a mean terminal
half-life of approximately 11 hours, consistent with preclinical species scaling. Steady state was
reached by day 4 of once-daily dosing, with an accumulation ratio of approximately 1.4.

## Conclusions

AXN-2401 demonstrated an acceptable safety and tolerability profile in healthy volunteers at doses
up to 150 mg once daily for 14 days, including no meaningful QTc prolongation on standard 12-lead
ECG, supporting advancement into a multiple-ascending-dose study in PF-7 patients with pathway
biomarker assessment.

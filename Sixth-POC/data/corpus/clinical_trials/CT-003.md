# AXN-2401-201: A Phase 2a Proof-of-Concept Efficacy Study in PF-7

## Design

Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, 24-week study of AXN-2401 150 mg once daily versus
placebo in 96 biomarker-confirmed PF-7 patients. Primary endpoint was change in forced vital
capacity (FVC) from baseline to week 24.

## Efficacy results

Patients treated with AXN-2401 showed a mean decline in FVC of 45 mL over 24 weeks, compared to a
mean decline of 165 mL in the placebo group (least-squares mean difference 120 mL, 95% CI 58-182
mL, p=0.002), representing a substantial slowing of disease progression relative to the natural
history of FVC decline previously characterized in the PF-7 subtype. Serum FBX9 decreased by a mean
of 54% in the AXN-2401 arm versus 3% in placebo, consistent with target engagement observed in the
prior Phase 1b study.

## Safety results

The safety profile was consistent with prior studies; adverse events were predominantly mild
gastrointestinal symptoms and headache. Two patients in the AXN-2401 arm and one in placebo
discontinued due to adverse events, none assessed as treatment-related cardiac events.

## Conclusions

This proof-of-concept study demonstrates a clinically meaningful and statistically significant
slowing of FVC decline with AXN-2401 in biomarker-confirmed PF-7 patients, providing the first
clinical efficacy evidence linking CRB3 pathway antagonism to a measurable benefit in this
patient population, and supporting progression to dose-ranging and larger confirmatory studies.

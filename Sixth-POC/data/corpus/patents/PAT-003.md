# CRB3 and FBX9 Biomarker Panel for Diagnosing PF-7

## Field of the invention

This patent discloses a diagnostic method and companion biomarker panel for identifying patients
with the PF-7 subtype of pulmonary fibrosis, based on combined measurement of lung tissue CRB3
expression and serum FBX9 concentration.

## Background

Prior classification of pulmonary fibrosis has relied primarily on radiographic and histological
pattern, without a molecular biomarker to identify patients whose disease is driven predominantly
by CRB3 pathway activity. A validated biomarker panel is needed to enrich clinical trial
populations for patients most likely to respond to CRB3-targeted therapy.

## Summary of the invention

The invention provides a method of diagnosing PF-7 comprising: measuring CRB3 immunohistochemistry
staining intensity in a lung tissue sample, measuring serum FBX9 concentration in a blood sample,
and classifying the patient as PF-7 if both measures exceed pre-specified thresholds derived from a
reference population.

## Claims (summary)

Claim 1 covers the combined CRB3 IHC plus serum FBX9 diagnostic method. Claim 4 recites specific
threshold values for CRB3 H-score and serum FBX9 concentration. Claim 8 claims use of the panel to
select patients for enrollment in a clinical trial of a CRB3 antagonist.

## Industrial applicability

This biomarker panel is disclosed as useful both for patient selection in a clinical trial of a
CRB3 antagonist such as AXN-2401 and, potentially, as a companion diagnostic accompanying
commercial use of such a therapy.

# Crystalline Salt Forms of AXN-2401 and Methods of Preparation

## Field of the invention

This patent discloses crystalline salt forms of AXN-2401, processes for their preparation, and
pharmaceutical compositions comprising these forms, addressing solid-state properties relevant to
manufacturing and shelf stability.

## Background

The free base form of AXN-2401 shows limited crystallinity and is hygroscopic, presenting
challenges for consistent tablet manufacturing and long-term storage stability. Identification of
a stable, well-defined crystalline salt form is desirable for commercial-scale production.

## Summary of the invention

The invention provides a crystalline hydrochloride salt of AXN-2401, designated Form A,
characterized by a specified X-ray powder diffraction pattern, and a process for its preparation
by crystallization from an ethanol/water solvent system. Form A shows less than 0.1% water uptake
at 60% relative humidity, in contrast to the hygroscopic free base.

## Claims (summary)

Claim 1 covers the crystalline hydrochloride salt (Form A) of AXN-2401 as characterized by the
disclosed X-ray powder diffraction pattern. Claim 6 covers the crystallization process. Claim 9
covers a pharmaceutical tablet composition comprising Form A.

## Industrial applicability

Form A is disclosed as the preferred solid form for commercial tablet manufacturing of AXN-2401
due to its superior physical stability relative to the free base, and is the form used in the
formulation development work described in the associated internal CMC report.

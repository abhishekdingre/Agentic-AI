# Combination Therapy of AXN-2401 with Standard-of-Care Antifibrotics

## Field of the invention

This patent discloses methods of treating pulmonary fibrosis by co-administering a CRB3 antagonist
such as AXN-2401 together with a standard-of-care antifibrotic agent acting through a distinct
mechanism, and pharmaceutical compositions for such combination use.

## Background

Standard-of-care antifibrotic agents act through tyrosine kinase inhibition or modulation of
fibrotic cytokine pathways, mechanisms distinct from CRB3 antagonism. Because AXN-2401 acts through
a parallel, non-overlapping pathway (the CRB3/FBX9 axis, which operates independently of the
canonical TGF-beta/SMAD axis targeted by existing agents), combination treatment is hypothesized to
produce additive antifibrotic benefit.

## Summary of the invention

The invention provides a method of treating pulmonary fibrosis, including PF-7, comprising
administering to a patient a therapeutically effective amount of AXN-2401 in combination with a
second antifibrotic agent, and pharmaceutical compositions comprising both active agents.

## Claims (summary)

Claim 1 covers the combination method. Claim 6 recites specific dose ranges for each agent when
co-administered. Claim 10 claims a fixed-dose combination pharmaceutical composition.

## Industrial applicability

This filing supports potential future combination clinical development; as of this filing, no
combination clinical trial data are available, and any efficacy benefit of combination therapy
described here is prospective and based on the proposed complementary mechanisms rather than on
completed clinical trial results.

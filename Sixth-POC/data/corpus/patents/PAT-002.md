# Method of Treating CRB3-Driven Pulmonary Fibrosis Subtypes with CRB3 Antagonists

## Field of the invention

This patent claims methods of treating a molecularly defined subtype of pulmonary fibrosis,
characterized by elevated CRB3 pathway activity, by administering a CRB3 antagonist such as
AXN-2401 to a patient identified as having that subtype.

## Background

Idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis is heterogeneous, and a subset of patients — referred to in this
specification as having "CRB3-high" or PF-7 disease — show elevated CRB3 receptor expression and
elevated serum FBX9, the downstream pathway biomarker, and experience more rapid disease
progression than the general idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis population.

## Summary of the invention

The invention provides a method comprising: (a) identifying a patient with pulmonary fibrosis as
having elevated CRB3 pathway activity, using serum FBX9 concentration, lung tissue CRB3
immunostaining, or both, above a specified threshold; and (b) administering to that patient a
therapeutically effective amount of a CRB3 antagonist, such as AXN-2401, to reduce fibrotic
progression.

## Claims (summary)

Claim 1 covers the method of identifying a PF-7 patient by serum FBX9 threshold and administering
a CRB3 antagonist. Claim 5 specifically recites AXN-2401 as the CRB3 antagonist administered.
Claim 9 recites a dosing regimen of once-daily oral administration. Claim 11 recites combination
administration with a second antifibrotic agent.

## Note on scope

This method claim is expressly limited to pulmonary fibrosis; the specification does not describe
or claim use of CRB3 antagonists, including AXN-2401, for renal fibrosis or any other
extrapulmonary fibrotic condition.

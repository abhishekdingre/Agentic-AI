# Composition of Matter: AXN-2401 and Related CRB3-Antagonist Compounds

## Field of the invention

This patent discloses a genus of pyrrolopyridine-carboxamide compounds that act as antagonists of
the CRB3 receptor, including the specific compound AXN-2401, and methods for their preparation.

## Background

CRB3 has been identified as a receptor implicated in fibrotic tissue remodeling. Existing
antifibrotic therapies do not directly target CRB3. There remains a need for potent, selective,
orally available small-molecule CRB3 antagonists.

## Summary of the invention

The invention provides compounds of general Formula I, a pyrrolopyridine core substituted with a
carboxamide side chain, wherein specified substituent positions confer CRB3 binding affinity in the
low nanomolar range. The specific compound designated AXN-2401 (Example 14 in this filing) is
claimed as a preferred embodiment, characterized by a measured CRB3 dissociation constant (Kd) of
approximately 3 nM and selectivity greater than 1000-fold over a panel of unrelated receptors.

## Claims (summary)

Claim 1 covers the genus of Formula I compounds. Claim 7 specifically claims AXN-2401 as a
composition of matter. Claim 12 claims pharmaceutically acceptable salts of AXN-2401, including
the crystalline hydrochloride salt form. Claim 15 claims pharmaceutical compositions comprising
AXN-2401 and a pharmaceutically acceptable excipient, formulated for oral administration.

## Industrial applicability

Compounds of Formula I, including AXN-2401, are disclosed as useful in the treatment of fibrotic
disease mediated by CRB3 signaling, with pulmonary fibrosis given as the principal exemplified
indication in the accompanying specification.
